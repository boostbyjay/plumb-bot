Woodland Hills is one of the San Fernando Valley's largest and most diverse communities. From the older residential neighborhoods near Warner Center to the newer developments in the western hills, from single-family homes to apartment complexes and the busy commercial district, Woodland Hills has plumbing needs that span the full spectrum.

## What Woodland Hills homeowners face

**Varied housing ages.** Woodland Hills has homes from every decade since the 1940s. Older homes near the center of the community have original galvanized pipes and clay sewer lines. Newer construction in the hills has modern PEX and PVC systems. Each needs different maintenance approaches.

**Valley hard water.** The San Fernando Valley's hard water takes a toll on every Woodland Hills plumbing system. Water heaters lose efficiency, fixtures get clogged, and appliance lifespan is reduced. Annual maintenance is the key to preventing costly failures.

**Warner Center commercial plumbing.** The Warner Center business district is one of the Valley's major commercial hubs. Office buildings, restaurants, retail spaces, and hotels all need commercial plumbing services — preventive maintenance, grease traps, backflow testing, and emergency response.

**Sewer line maintenance.** Many Woodland Hills homes have clay or cast iron sewer lines that have been in the ground for decades. Tree roots from mature landscaping infiltrate joints. Camera inspections catch problems early.

## Tips for Woodland Hills homeowners

- Flush your water heater annually
- Schedule sewer inspections for homes over 30 years old
- Commercial property managers at Warner Center: invest in preventive maintenance contracts
- Track your water bill for unexplained increases
