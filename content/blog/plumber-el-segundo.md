El Segundo is a unique South Bay city — part residential community, part industrial corridor, and entirely adjacent to LAX. The city's plumbing needs reflect that mix: family homes that need the same care as any LA County property, and commercial/industrial facilities that need specialized plumbing expertise.

## El Segundo's residential plumbing

**Mid-century homes.** Most of El Segundo's residential neighborhoods have homes built in the 1940s through 1960s. Original galvanized supply piping, cast iron drain stacks, and clay sewer lines are common. These systems need regular maintenance and eventual replacement as they age past their intended lifespan.

**Coastal proximity.** El Segundo is one block from the ocean. Salt air corrosion affects exterior plumbing components, outdoor faucets, and any exposed copper or brass. More frequent inspections and corrosion-resistant materials are essential.

**Hard water.** El Segundo shares LA County's hard water supply. Water heater maintenance, fixture protection, and annual flushing are standard practices for local homeowners.

## El Segundo's commercial and industrial plumbing

El Segundo's industrial corridor — built around the aerospace and aviation industries — includes facilities with specialized plumbing needs. Commercial kitchens, industrial drain systems, grease traps for facility cafeterias, and large-scale water heating systems all need commercial-grade plumbing expertise.

## Tips for El Segundo homeowners

- Annual water heater flushing is essential in this hard water area
- Inspect exterior plumbing for salt air corrosion annually
- Schedule sewer inspections for older homes
