Baldwin Park is a solid, family-focused community in the heart of the San Gabriel Valley. The homes here were built primarily in the 1950s and 1960s, and while they were well-constructed for their time, 60-plus years of plumbing service takes a toll. Baldwin Park homeowners deal with the same San Gabriel Valley-wide challenges — hard water, aging infrastructure, and clay sewer lines — combined with the specific realities of mid-century home construction.

## What Baldwin Park homeowners should know

**Mid-century plumbing systems.** Homes built in the 1950s and 60s often have original galvanized piping and cast iron drain stacks. Galvanized pipes corrode from the inside, gradually reducing water pressure and eventually requiring replacement. Cast iron drain lines can last a long time, but root intrusion and internal corrosion eventually cause problems.

**Hard water throughout the SGV.** Baldwin Park shares the San Gabriel Valley's hard water supply. Sediment builds up in water heaters, reducing efficiency and shortening lifespan. Aerators and showerheads get clogged with mineral deposits. Over time, scale builds up inside pipes.

**Clay sewer lines.** Many Baldwin Park homes still have clay sewer mains. Tree roots from mature landscaping infiltrate joints, causing slow drains, backups, and eventually pipe failure. If you have recurring clogs in the same drain, a sewer camera inspection can identify root intrusion before it becomes a full blockage.

**Water heater replacements.** If your water heater is over 10 years old, it's time to start planning for replacement. Hard water in Baldwin Park can reduce a water heater's lifespan by several years compared to areas with softer water.

## When to call a professional

Multiple drains backing up simultaneously, no hot water after checking the water heater, a water bill that's jumped without explanation, or any sign of sewage smell indoors — those are the moments to call a licensed plumber.
