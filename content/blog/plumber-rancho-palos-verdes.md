Rancho Palos Verdes sits on the Palos Verdes Peninsula's interior hillsides — rolling terrain, ocean-view estates, and some of the most expensive real estate in LA County. The homes here are large, the lots are generous, and the plumbing challenges are as unique as the properties themselves.

## RPV's plumbing challenges

**Hillside terrain.** Like the rest of the Peninsula, RPV homes are built on slopes. Sewer lines run long distances downhill, water pressure varies by elevation, and soil movement stresses underground pipes. Hillside plumbing work requires equipment and expertise beyond standard residential jobs.

**Coastal salt air.** Even though RPV is set back from the ocean, salt air still reaches the community. Exterior plumbing components, outdoor faucets, and irrigation systems face accelerated corrosion.

**Large custom estates.** RPV properties tend to be large — 3,000-6,000+ square feet — with multiple bathrooms, pool and spa systems, outdoor kitchens, and complex drain layouts. These systems need a plumber experienced with high-end residential work.

**Seismic activity.** The Palos Verdes Peninsula is an area of elevated seismic risk. Earthquake-safe plumbing — strapped water heaters, flexible gas connectors, seismic valves — is essential for RPV homes.
