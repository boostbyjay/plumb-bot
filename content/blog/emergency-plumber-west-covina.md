West Covina sits in the center of the San Gabriel Valley, and its plumbing reflects that mix of established neighborhoods and newer developments. Homes built in the 1950s and 1960s make up a large part of the housing stock, and while those mid-century builds tend to be solid, their plumbing wasn't designed to last 70-plus years without attention. When an emergency strikes in West Covina — at any hour — you need a plumber who knows the area and can respond quickly.

## Plumbing emergencies we see in West Covina

**Water heater failures.** The San Gabriel Valley's hard water is rough on water heaters. A sediment-filled tank can fail without warning, leaving a West Covina family with no hot water — or worse, a leaking tank that floods the garage or utility room.

**Burst pipes.** Older copper and galvanized pipes develop weak spots over time, especially where the pipe bends or connects to fittings. Pressure spikes, freezing temperatures, or simple age can cause a pipe to burst. In West Covina's older homes, these failures often happen in walls or under slab foundations where you can't see them until the damage is done.

**Sewer line backups.** Clay sewer lines are still present in many West Covina homes. Tree roots from mature landscaping find their way into joints and cracks, and the result is a backup that affects every drain in the house. If your toilet, shower, and sink are all draining slowly at the same time, the main line is the likely culprit.

**Toilet overflows and drain backups.** A clogged toilet is manageable. But when the overflow is caused by a main sewer line blockage, plunging only makes things worse by pushing the blockage further down. Knowing the difference between a simple clog and a main line issue is critical.

## What to keep in your West Covina home

- Know where your main water shutoff valve is (usually near the water meter at the front of the property or in the garage)
- Keep a bucket, plunger, and adjustable wrench accessible
- Know the location of your water heater's shutoff valve
- Save a reliable plumber's number in your phone before you need one

## The cost of waiting

Every minute counts in a plumbing emergency. A small leak can release hundreds of gallons of water into your home in a matter of hours. The cost of water damage cleanup — drywall, flooring, personal property — far exceeds the cost of calling a plumber immediately.
