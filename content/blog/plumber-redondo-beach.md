Redondo Beach has the perfect Southern California lifestyle — a famous pier, beachfront properties, a charming downtown, and family neighborhoods that feel like a small town despite being in the middle of LA County's South Bay. But that coastal location comes with plumbing challenges that inland homeowners don't have to deal with.

## Redondo Beach plumbing challenges

**Salt air corrosion.** Homes near the beach in Redondo experience faster corrosion on exterior plumbing. Outdoor faucets, irrigation systems, copper piping in exterior walls, and pool equipment all need more frequent inspection and maintenance. Corrosion-resistant materials are a must for any outdoor plumbing work.

**Older homes near the pier and downtown.** The area around the Redondo Beach Pier, the downtown commercial district, and residential streets within a few blocks of the ocean has homes built in the 1940s through 1960s. Original galvanized piping, aging water heaters, and clay sewer lines are common.

**Vacation rental stress.** Redondo Beach has a significant vacation rental market, particularly near the beach. High turnover means plumbing systems get stressed more than in primary residences. Fixture failures and drain clogs are more frequent in rental properties.

**High water table.** Proximity to the ocean means a higher water table in many Redondo Beach areas. This can affect slab foundations, basement and crawlspace conditions, and sump pump needs.

## Tips for Redondo Beach homeowners

- Inspect exterior plumbing annually for corrosion
- Flush your water heater every 6 months (salt air + hard water = extra stress)
- Keep outdoor faucet washers and washers replaced
- Schedule sewer inspections for older homes
