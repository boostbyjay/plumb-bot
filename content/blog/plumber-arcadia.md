Arcadia is one of the San Gabriel Valley's premier residential communities — larger lot sizes, mature homes, and a reputation for quality that attracts homeowners who take property maintenance seriously. But those same qualities create specific plumbing challenges. Arcadia's homes tend to be bigger, older, and surrounded by the kind of mature landscaping that puts stress on underground plumbing.

## What makes Arcadia plumbing different

**Mature tree root systems.** Arcadia is known for its large, established trees — oaks, sycamores, pine, and palm. These trees have deep, aggressive root systems that seek out water, and old clay sewer lines are an irresistible target. Root intrusion is one of the most common sewer line problems in Arcadia, and it's often the cause of recurring drain clogs, slow drains, and sewage backups.

**Larger homes, more complex plumbing.** Arcadia homes are often 2,500 to 4,000+ square feet with multiple bathrooms, larger water heaters, and more complex drain systems. More plumbing means more potential failure points. A leak that goes unnoticed in a smaller home can cause significantly more damage in a larger property.

**Older sewer infrastructure.** Many Arcadia homes still have clay sewer mains running from the street. These pipes were built to last, but 50-70 years of root intrusion, ground movement, and corrosion takes its toll. Trenchless sewer repair has become a popular option in Arcadia because it avoids destroying mature landscaping and hardscaping.

**Hard water.** The SGV's hard water affects every home in Arcadia. Water heater efficiency drops, fixtures develop scale, and appliances run less efficiently over time.

## Arcadia maintenance tips

- Schedule a sewer camera inspection every 2 years if your home is over 40 years old
- Consider a water softener for long-term hard water protection
- Keep an eye on your water bill — sudden increases often signal hidden leaks
- Flush your water heater annually
