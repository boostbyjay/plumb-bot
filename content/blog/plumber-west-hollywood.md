West Hollywood is one of the densest cities in LA County — a mix of multi-unit apartment buildings, mid-century bungalows, luxury condos along the Sunset Strip, and commercial properties that never close. That density creates plumbing needs that are different from any other part of the county.

## WeHo's plumbing landscape

**Multi-unit apartment buildings.** WeHo has a massive inventory of apartment buildings, from garden-style complexes to high-rises. These buildings need a commercial plumber who can handle shared infrastructure — main water lines, central water heaters, sewer mains, and after-hours emergency calls from tenants.

**Historic bungalows.** The residential neighborhoods away from the commercial corridors have many 1920s-1940s bungalows. Original galvanized pipes, cast iron drain stacks, and clay sewer lines are common. These homes need careful plumbing upgrades that preserve their character.

**Luxury condos and the Sunset Strip.** High-end condos along Sunset Boulevard and in the Hills area have modern plumbing systems that still need professional maintenance. Water heaters, fixture upgrades, and preventive maintenance keep these properties running smoothly.

**24/7 commercial demand.** WeHo's nightlife and entertainment industry means plumbing emergencies don't follow business hours. Restaurants, bars, hotels, and entertainment venues need a plumber who responds on nights and weekends.

## Short-term rental properties**

WeHo has a high concentration of short-term rentals. High turnover means heavy fixture usage and deferred maintenance between guests. Property managers need a plumber who can handle quick turnarounds and emergency calls.
