El Monte is one of the oldest communities in Los Angeles County, and its plumbing reflects decades of honest, practical homeownership. The homes here — many built in the 1940s, 50s, and 60s — are solid but aging. El Monte's working-class neighborhoods need plumbing services that are honest, affordable, and get the job done right the first time.

## Common El Monte plumbing issues

**Aging galvanized and cast iron pipes.** Many El Monte homes still have galvanized supply lines and cast iron drain stacks from original construction. Galvanized pipes corrode from the inside, narrowing over time and reducing water pressure. Cast iron drain lines last a long time but eventually develop corrosion and root intrusion.

**Water heater failures.** The San Gabriel Valley's hard water is brutal on water heaters. In El Monte homes where water heaters have been in service for 15+ years, the risk of tank failure is real. A failed water heater can flood a garage or utility room in minutes.

**Sewer line backups.** Clay sewer lines are still present in many El Monte homes. Tree roots and ground movement cause blockages that affect every drain in the house simultaneously. If your toilet, shower, and sink are all draining slowly, it's almost certainly a main line problem.

**Drain clogs from kitchen grease.** Kitchen drain clogs from accumulated cooking grease are one of the most frequent service calls in El Monte. Prevention is simple — run hot water and dish soap down the drain after cooking, and avoid pouring grease down the drain entirely.

## Tips for El Monte homeowners

- Flush your water heater annually
- Never pour grease down the kitchen drain
- Schedule a sewer camera inspection for homes over 40 years old
- Know where your main shutoff valve is
