Burbank is best known as the media capital of the world, but for its homeowners and business owners, plumbing emergencies don't wait for production schedules. Whether you live in a historic Burbank bungalow near Magnolia Park, a mid-century home in the hills, or a newer build near the studio district, a plumbing emergency is the last thing you need on a busy day.

## Common Burbank plumbing emergencies

**Water heater failures.** Burbank's water comes from the LA Department of Water and Power and is moderately hard. Over time, sediment builds up at the bottom of the tank, reducing heating efficiency and eventually causing the system to fail. A water heater that dies in the middle of winter is every Burbank homeowner's worst scenario.

**Burst pipes.** Older Burbank homes, especially those in the Magnolia Park and equestrian areas, often have original copper or galvanized pipes that have been in service for 50+ years. Corrosion, pressure spikes, and temperature changes can all cause pipes to fail. A burst pipe in a wall or ceiling can cause extensive water damage in a matter of hours.

**Sewer line backups.** Clay sewer lines are common in Burbank homes built before the 1970s. Tree roots from the area's mature sycamores, oaks, and fruit trees infiltrate joints and cause blockages. If you have multiple drains draining slowly or sewage backing up into floor drains, the main sewer line is likely the issue.

**Drain clogs.** Kitchen grease clogs, bathroom hair clogs, and laundry detergent buildup can all cause serious drain problems. In older Burbank homes with narrower pipes, clogs happen more frequently and are harder to clear without professional equipment.

## What Burbank homeowners should know

- **Permits matter.** Burbank has specific plumbing permit requirements. Always hire a licensed plumber who pulls the necessary permits — it protects your home value and ensures the work is inspected.
- **Water pressure.** Homes in Burbank's hillside areas sometimes experience low water pressure due to elevation. A pressure-boosting system may be necessary if you're installing new fixtures or appliances.
- **Root intrusion is common.** If you have large trees near your sewer line, schedule a camera inspection every few years. Catching root intrusion early means trenchless repair instead of a full line replacement.

## When to call

If you have active flooding, sewage backing up, no hot water during cold weather, or a gas smell near your water heater — don't wait. These are emergencies.
