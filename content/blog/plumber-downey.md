Downey is one of LA County's most established cities, and its neighborhoods reflect decades of steady growth. From the older homes near downtown Downey to the newer construction in the northern and western parts of the city, Downey's plumbing systems span nearly every era of Southern California home building.

## Downey's common plumbing challenges

**Aging supply lines.** Many Downey homes built before 1970 still have galvanized supply piping. Inside diameter narrows from mineral deposits and corrosion, leading to low water pressure and eventual failure. If your Downey home has gradually decreasing water pressure, galvanized pipes are the likely cause.

**Sewer line issues.** Clay sewer lines are present in many older Downey homes. Root intrusion from mature trees causes blockages that affect every drain in the house simultaneously. A sewer camera inspection is the fastest way to diagnose main line problems.

**Water heater replacements.** Hard water in the LA County area shortens water heater lifespan. If your water heater is over 10 years old, start planning for replacement before it fails. A tank failure can flood a garage or utility room in minutes and cause thousands in water damage.

**Drain cleaning.** Kitchen grease clogs, bathroom hair clogs, and laundry detergent buildup cause regular drain problems in Downey homes. Hydro jetting is the most effective long-term solution for homes with persistent clog issues.

## Tips for Downey homeowners

- Flush your water heater annually
- Don't pour grease down the kitchen drain
- Schedule sewer inspections for older homes
- Keep a plumber's number saved before you need it
