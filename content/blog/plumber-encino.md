Encino is one of the San Fernando Valley's most established family communities — large lot sizes, good schools, and homes that were built to be lived in for generations. Most Encino homes date from the 1950s through the 1980s, and while they were well-built, 40-70 years of plumbing service takes its toll.

## Encino's common plumbing issues

**Aging galvanized and copper pipes.** Encino homes from the 1950s and 60s often have original galvanized supply lines. Inside corrosion gradually reduces water pressure and eventually leads to leaks or complete pipe failure. Later construction used copper, which lasts longer but can still develop pinhole leaks from pH imbalance in the water supply.

**Valley hard water.** The San Fernando Valley has some of the hardest water in LA County. Water heaters in Encino homes battle constant sediment buildup. Annual flushing is essential to maintain efficiency and extend the unit's lifespan.

**Sewer line challenges.** Clay sewer lines are common in Encino homes. The Valley's mature oak, sycamore, and pine trees have aggressive root systems that infiltrate sewer line joints. If you have recurring drain clogs, gurgling sounds, or slow drains across multiple fixtures, the main sewer line needs inspection.

**Large home, big water heaters.** Encino homes tend to be 2,000+ square feet with multiple bathrooms. These homes need appropriately sized water heaters — and when those units fail, the replacement needs to handle the demand.

## Encino maintenance tips

- Flush your water heater annually
- Schedule sewer camera inspections every few years
- Consider a water softener for long-term hard water protection
- Know where your main shutoff valve is
