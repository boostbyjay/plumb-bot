Cerritos consistently ranks as one of the best places to live in LA County — excellent schools, well-kept neighborhoods, and a community that values quality home maintenance. The plumbing in Cerritos homes reflects that standard of care, but even well-maintained systems need professional attention as they age.

## Cerritos plumbing landscape

**Primarily 1960s-1980s construction.** Most Cerritos homes were built during the city's major development periods. Homes from the 1960s and early 70s may still have original galvanized supply lines and cast iron drain stacks. Later construction used copper and eventually PEX, which are more durable but still need maintenance.

**Hard water effects.** Cerritos shares the LA County hard water supply. Water heaters battle sediment buildup, fixtures get clogged with mineral deposits, and appliance lifespan is reduced. Annual water heater flushing is one of the simplest and most effective maintenance tasks a Cerritos homeowner can perform.

**Sewer line maintenance.** Many Cerritos homes have clay or cast iron sewer lines that have been in the ground for 40-50 years. Tree roots from mature neighborhood landscaping find their way into joints and cracks. A sewer camera inspection every few years catches problems early.

**Gas line safety.** Some Cerritos homes have older gas lines serving water heaters, furnaces, and fireplaces. If you're experiencing issues with gas appliances, have a licensed plumber inspect the gas line for corrosion or leaks.

## Tips for Cerritos homeowners

- Flush your water heater annually
- Schedule sewer inspections every 2-3 years for homes over 30 years old
- Consider a water softener for long-term hard water protection
- Keep your main shutoff valve location marked
