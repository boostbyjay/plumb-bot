Rolling Hills isn't just a city — it's a private, gated community with some of the largest and most valuable residential properties in Los Angeles County. Equestrian trails, custom estates on multi-acre lots, and a commitment to preserving the area's rural character make Rolling Hills one of a kind. The plumbing systems in these properties are equally unique, and they demand the kind of expertise that comes from working on high-end residential projects.

## What makes Rolling Hills plumbing different

**Multi-acre properties with long sewer runs.** Rolling Hills lots are measured in acres, not square feet. Sewer lines from the house to the street can be 200-500+ feet long, often running through terrain that's difficult to access. These aren't standard repair jobs — they require equipment like trenchless pipe lining that avoids destroying landscaping and driveways.

**Large custom estates.** Rolling Hills homes are typically 4,000-8,000+ square feet with multiple bathrooms, guest houses, pool and spa systems, equestrian facility plumbing, and extensive outdoor systems. Water heating alone often requires multiple units or a large commercial-grade system.

**Equine and agricultural plumbing.** Some Rolling Hills properties have barns, tack rooms, and wash racks with their own plumbing needs. Outdoor faucets, water lines for horse troughs, and drainage systems for arenas and paddocks add another layer of complexity.

**Discretion and security.** Rolling Hills is a private community with strict access controls. Plumbers need to be professional, respectful of privacy, and trustworthy — homeowners here expect the same level of quality from their service providers as they do from their homes.

## Services Rolling Hills homeowners need

- Sewer line inspection and trenchless repair for long property runs
- Water heater systems for large estates
- Whole-home repiping
- Pool, spa, and outdoor plumbing
- Preventive maintenance programs
- Emergency response for large properties
