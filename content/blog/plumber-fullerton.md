Fullerton is one of Orange County's most diverse cities when it comes to plumbing. You've got Cal State Fullerton students cycling through rental properties near campus, historic homes in the Downtown Fullerton district dating back to the late 1800s, mid-century ranches in Sunny Hills and Troy, and commercial buildings along Harbor Boulevard and Commonwealth Avenue. Each of these property types brings its own set of plumbing challenges.

## What makes Fullerton plumbing unique

**Rental property stress.** The area around Cal State Fullerton and Fullerton College has a high concentration of student rental housing. These properties see heavy usage and often suffer from neglect — deferred maintenance, cheap fixture replacements, and landlords who don't address issues until they become emergencies. If you're a tenant or a property owner in this area, staying on top of plumbing maintenance is essential.

**Older infrastructure in historic districts.** Downtown Fullerton and the surrounding historic neighborhoods have homes and commercial buildings that date back to the early 1900s. Original cast iron pipes, galvanized supply lines, and clay sewer mains are common. These systems need a plumber who knows how to work on older plumbing without causing more damage.

**Water heater demands.** Fullerton's hard water, shared across much of Orange County, takes a toll on water heaters. In rental properties where multiple tenants cycle through, water heaters often get pushed past their intended lifespan. Sediment buildup from hard water reduces efficiency and shortens the unit's life.

## Tips for Fullerton homeowners and property owners

- If you own rental property near campus, schedule annual plumbing inspections between tenant turnovers
- Homes in historic Fullerton should have sewer line camera inspections every 2-3 years
- Flush your water heater annually to combat Orange County's hard water
- Know where your main shutoff valve is — apartment and rental tenants should ask their landlord

Look for a licensed California plumber with experience in both residential and rental properties. Fullerton has specific municipal codes and permit requirements that vary from other OC cities.
