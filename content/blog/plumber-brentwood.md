Brentwood is one of the Westside's premier family communities — large lot sizes, well-maintained homes, top-rated schools, and a location that's close to everything without being in the middle of it. The homes here tend to be larger and newer than what you find in older LA neighborhoods, but that doesn't make them maintenance-free.

## Brentwood plumbing considerations

**1960s-1980s construction.** Most Brentwood homes were built between the 1960s and 1980s. While newer than LA's historic neighborhoods, these homes are now 40-60 years old. Original copper piping has decades of service, water heaters from the original construction are long overdue for replacement, and sewer lines have been in the ground since the house was built.

**Hard water on the Westside.** Brentwood shares LA's hard water supply. Mineral buildup in water heaters reduces efficiency and shortens lifespan. Scale on fixtures affects appearance and flow. Annual water heater flushing is essential.

**Large homes, complex systems.** Brentwood homes are typically 2,000-4,000+ square feet with multiple bathrooms, larger water heaters, and complex drain systems. More plumbing means more potential failure points. A whole-home inspection can identify weak spots before they become emergencies.

**Mature tree root systems.** Brentwood's established neighborhoods have mature trees with aggressive root systems. If your home has a clay or cast iron sewer line, root intrusion is a risk that grows with every passing year.

## Tips for Brentwood homeowners

- Flush your water heater annually
- Schedule a sewer camera inspection every few years for homes over 30 years old
- Consider upgrading to a tankless water heater for efficiency and endless hot water
- Keep your main shutoff valve location marked
