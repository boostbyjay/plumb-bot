## Commercial plumbing services Pasadena businesses need

**Grease trap service.** Pasadena's restaurant scene is thriving, and every commercial kitchen needs a properly maintained grease trap. California health code is strict about grease trap pumping schedules, and non-compliance can lead to health inspection failures, fines, or temporary closure. Scheduled pumping service keeps your kitchen compliant and your drains flowing.

**Backflow testing and certification.** Pasadena requires annual backflow testing for commercial properties. If you own or manage a restaurant, office building, medical practice, or multi-tenant property in Pasadena, your backflow prevention device needs to be tested and certified every year. Missing the deadline means penalties from the Pasadena water department.

**Multi-unit property maintenance.** Pasadena has numerous apartment complexes and condo buildings, each with its own plumbing challenges. **Preventive maintenance contracts.** The businesses that stay out of emergency situations are the ones that invest in preventive maintenance. Scheduled inspections, water heater servicing, drain cleaning, and fixture checks keep your plumbing systems running reliably and extend their lifespan.
